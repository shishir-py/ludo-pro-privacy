Ludo Pro Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 7, 2026

In short: Ludo Pro does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. Everything the app saves stays on your device.

Information we collect

None. Ludo Pro has no user accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no crash-reporting services. The app does not request internet access, location, contacts, camera, microphone or any other personal information.

Information stored on your device

To work properly, the app saves the following data locally on your device only:

  • Your settings (sound, haptics, game speed, board style and the player name you type in).
  • Your game statistics (games played, wins, captures and similar counts).
  • An in-progress game, so you can continue later.

This data never leaves your device. You can erase your statistics at any time from Settings → Reset statistics, and uninstalling the app removes all of its data.

Sharing with third parties

We do not share any data with third parties, because we do not collect any.

Children's privacy

Ludo Pro is suitable for players of all ages. Because the app collects no personal information, it does not collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, please contact the developer using the email address shown on Ludo Pro's Google Play store listing.
